# Viral Engine Analytics — Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 25, 2026

## Scope
Viral Engine Analytics is a local desktop application currently used for internal testing and management of authorized social media accounts. This policy describes its handling of information, including information received through TikTok authorization.

## Information processed
Depending on granted permissions, the application may process account identifiers, display names, profile information, account statistics (including follower and video counts), public video identifiers and links, video statistics (views, likes, comments and shares), publication records, and authorization tokens. It also processes locally supplied content and editorial metadata. It does not require TikTok passwords or request TikTok posting permissions.

## Purposes
Information is used to authenticate authorized accounts, associate publication records with the correct account and video, display available performance statistics, maintain permitted historical records and diagnose integration failures. TikTok data is used only for the authorized functionality and subject to TikTok's applicable terms and permissions.

## Storage and security
Application records and metric snapshots are stored locally in SQLite on the operator's computer. Authorization secrets and tokens are stored in the macOS Keychain. Local backups may contain application records. Access is limited to the authorized operator. No security measure guarantees absolute protection.

## Disclosure and third parties
The application communicates with authorized platform APIs for the requested integration. It does not sell personal data or use TikTok data for advertising or model training. Connected platforms process data under their own privacy policies. Information may be disclosed if legally required.

## Retention, deletion and withdrawal
Records should be retained only as needed for the authorized purposes and for periods permitted by applicable platform terms and law. The operator can stop synchronization and revoke access through the relevant platform's account settings. To request access, correction or deletion of personal information, contact the address below. Requests are handled subject to applicable law; deletion includes relevant local records and backups as appropriate. Revoking authorization stops future authorized access but does not by itself erase previously stored local records.

## Rights and contact
Applicable privacy rights, including those under Brazil's General Data Protection Law (LGPD), may be exercised by contacting the application operator at esefossereal.media@gmail.com. This address also handles privacy questions and withdrawal requests.

## Changes
This policy may be updated as application functionality or applicable requirements change. The effective date will be updated accordingly.
